Questions

Before you come in

What should I bring to the first visit?

Previous ECGs, echo or angiography reports, discharge summaries and every medicine you take, in the original packets if possible.

Do I need to fast for the tests?

No fasting is needed for an ECG or echo. If blood tests are advised, you will be told how to prepare when you book.

How soon will I get my reports?

ECG and echo reports are ready the same day. Holter and ambulatory BP results follow within two days.

When should I go to the emergency room instead?

Go straight to the nearest emergency department for chest pain lasting more than a few minutes, sudden breathlessness, fainting or a racing heart with dizziness.
